# PRD — research workstream (Nightlife Autopilot)

Status: planning draft, 2026-11-11 (updated for CONTRACTS v2).
Deliverables due to integration: **Fri Nov 13 18:00 ET** (CONTRACTS v2 §I); demo Nov 17. Owner: research subtab (project owner Ryan Mehta).
Branch: `nightlife/research`. Builds into `research/` (per `docs/CONTRACTS.md`).

## 1. Problem

Nightlife Autopilot claims that groups in the LES can't tell which bar is good
*right now*, and that existing tools give stale or static answers. That claim
is untested. We have a public-data venue DB (5,737 venues, 35 launch venues in
10002) and complaint-risk models (ROC AUC ~0.72), but no evidence about:

- whether the "where do we go right now" pain is real, frequent and unsolved;
- what people use today (Google Maps popular times, Instagram stories, group
  chats, Partiful, Resy, DICE, word of mouth) and why past apps (BarHop, Buzzd,
  nYtevibe, What's Playin, BarHopper) didn't stick;
- whether anyone would act as a Scout for points, and whether venue operators
  would pay for a claimed profile, offers or demand analytics.

The research workstream answers these, gives the team a positioning statement
and pricing hypotheses, and — because real interviews will lag the Nov 17
deadline — ships a clearly marked synthetic interview/survey dataset with the
same shape the real data will have, so tableau/frontend/coursework can build on
it now and swap later.

## 2. Goals

1. **G1 Competitive refresh**: current status (live / dead / pivoted, last
   update, platform, model) of ≥ 10 competitors and substitutes, with sources
   and retrieval dates.
2. **G2 Competitive matrix + positioning**: a feature × competitor matrix on
   the dimensions that matter to our three scores (vibe, match, confidence) and
   a one-paragraph positioning statement.
3. **G3 Interview guide**: consumer (groups), Scout and venue-operator guides
   for 10-15 interviews total, with screener, consent script and note template.
4. **G4 Survey design**: a ≤ 5-minute survey (~20 questions) with a codebook
   that maps 1:1 to the dataset schema.
5. **G5 Synthetic dataset**: `syn_interviews`, `syn_interview_quotes`,
   `syn_survey_responses` generated by a seeded script, marked synthetic per
   the contract, and registered in `docs/synthetic/research.md`.
6. **G6 Business model**: venue pricing hypotheses (tiers, price points as
   placeholders), consumer/Scout incentive economics, and the test that would
   validate each.
7. **G7 Hand-offs**: one `research/summary.md` with the 5-10 findings other
   workstreams should act on (e.g. default weights, which filters matter,
   badge wording).

## 3. Non-goals

- No application code in backend/frontend/recs; research outputs are docs,
  CSVs and one generator script.
- No scraping of Instagram, Google, Yelp, Resy or DICE (ToS). Competitor info
  comes from public pages, app-store listings and press, cited by URL + date.
- No claim that synthetic interview findings are real. Synthetic results may be
  charted for demo purposes only, always badged "demo data".
- No statistically powered study; 10-15 interviews and a convenience survey
  are directional.
- No IRB process (class project, no sensitive data); we still collect consent
  and store no names/contacts in the repo.

## 4. Users of this workstream's output

| User | What they need from research |
|---|---|
| Project team / instructor | Evidence the problem is real; positioning; pricing story for the final presentation |
| specs | Which preference fields matter most; freshness tolerance ("how old is too old?") to inform half-life |
| ml-recs | Rank of decision factors (line, cover, music, crowd, distance) → default match weights |
| frontend | Which filters/badges to show first; trust cues that raise confidence |
| tableau | Tidy CSVs (survey + interview codes) for a "voice of customer" dashboard |
| coursework | A labeled dataset (e.g. survey → "would use weekly" / churn-like target) |
| Real interviewees (later) | Short, respectful interview; consent; no PII stored |

Personas being researched (product users): **Group planner** (21-30, picks
the spot for 3-6 friends), **Scout** (out 2+ nights/week, likes status/perks),
**Venue operator** (owner/GM/promoter of an LES bar or club).

## 5. User stories with acceptance criteria

**R1 — As the team, I want a current competitor list, so we don't repeat dead
ideas.**
- AC: `research/competitors.csv` has ≥ 10 rows incl. BarHop, Buzzd, nYtevibe,
  What's Playin, BarHopper, Google Maps (popular times / live busyness),
  Instagram, Partiful, Resy, DICE (+ ≥ 2 more, e.g. Yelp, Beli, Posh, Fever,
  Eventbrite, TikTok, Snap Map).
- AC: every row has `status`, `last_seen_active`, `source_url`, `retrieved_at`;
  unknown values are `unknown`, never guessed.
- AC: each defunct product has a one-line "why it likely failed" hypothesis
  labeled as a hypothesis.

**R2 — As the presenter, I want a competitive matrix and positioning
statement.**
- AC: `research/competitive_matrix.md` scores each competitor on ≥ 8 dimensions
  (real-time signal, crowd-sourced, group matching, confidence shown,
  venue-side tools, LES coverage, incentives for reporters, cost to consumer)
  with Y / partial / N and a footnote per non-obvious cell.
- AC: positioning statement in the standard "For … who … unlike … we …" form,
  ≤ 60 words, plus a 2×2 chart spec (axes: freshness × personalization).

**R3 — As an interviewer, I want guides I can run in 30 minutes.**
- AC: three guides (consumer 25-30 min, Scout 20 min, operator 25 min), each
  with screener, consent script, warm-up, core questions (past behavior, not
  hypotheticals), concept test, and wrap-up.
- AC: every core question maps to ≥ 1 hypothesis ID (H1…Hn) in the guide.
- AC: a note template whose fields equal the `interviews` schema.

**R4 — As the team, I want a survey that feeds the dashboard directly.**
- AC: `research/survey/questions.md` ≤ 22 questions, ≤ 5 min median.
- AC: `research/survey/codebook.csv` lists every question → column name, type,
  allowed values; the synthetic CSV validates against it.

**R5 — As tableau/coursework, I want data now.**
- AC: `python -I research/gen_synthetic.py --seed 42` writes the three CSVs to
  `research/synthetic/` deterministically (same seed → identical bytes). Per
  CONTRACTS v2 §E these tables stay in `research/` and are **not** loaded into
  any DB (`manhattan_bars.db` or `live_sim.db`).
- AC: synthetic primary keys start with `syn-` (v2 §D): `syn-int-001`,
  `syn-q-0001`, `syn-srv-0001`. Real rows use `int-r-001`, `srv-r-0001`.
- AC: every row `is_synthetic = 1`; `docs/synthetic/research.md` lists each
  dataset with the 5 required fields.
- AC: interview `venue_mentions` use real launch `venue_id`s only.

**R6 — As the presenter, I want venue pricing hypotheses.**
- AC: `research/business_model.md` with ≥ 3 tiers (free claimed profile, paid
  Pro, per-offer/performance), placeholder prices marked `[PLACEHOLDER]`, unit
  economics table, and for each hypothesis a falsification test and threshold.

**R7 — As other workstreams, I want a findings summary.**
- AC: `research/summary.md` with ≤ 10 findings, each tagged
  `[SYNTHETIC]` or `[REAL n=…]` and pointing to the consuming workstream.

## 7. Requirements

Functional
- F1 Competitor dataset with sourced fields and retrieval dates.
- F2 Matrix + positioning markdown; 2×2 spec (tableau may render it).
- F3 Interview guides, screener, consent, note template.
- F4 Survey questions + codebook; export format = CSV matching codebook.
- F5 Seeded synthetic generator (Python, stdlib + numpy/pandas only) writing
  CSVs only (no DB load, v2 §E). Genre options use the shared vocabulary
  (v2.1 §E: 17 slugs in `simulator/config/genres.json`) — read from that JSON when present, else a copied constant that a test checks against it.
- F6 Validator: `research/validate.py` checks CSVs vs codebook/schema,
  `is_synthetic` present, venue_ids ∈ launch list.
- F7 Business model doc; findings summary.

Non-functional
- NF1 Deterministic, runs in < 10 s, no network.
- NF2 No PII: real interview IDs are `int-r-###`, no names/emails/phones in repo.
- NF3 Every synthetic artifact is discoverable by `grep -r is_synthetic` and
  `grep -r "SYNTHETIC:"`.
- NF4 Does not modify `src/`, any `.db` file, or other folders.

## 9. Success metrics

- Desk research: ≥ 10 competitors with 100% of rows sourced and dated.
- Guides reviewed by ≥ 1 teammate; pilot interview ≤ 30 min.
- Synthetic data: validator passes; 0 rows without `is_synthetic`; 0 unknown venue_ids.
- Real data (stretch/later): ≥ 10 interviews (≥ 6 consumers, ≥ 2 Scouts-type,
  ≥ 2 operators); survey n ≥ 100 `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- Decision metrics the research will report (thresholds are placeholders):
  - Pain: ≥ 60% of target respondents report a "bad pick" in the last month.
  - Scout supply: ≥ 20% say they'd submit ≥ 1 report/night for perks.
  - Operator WTP: ≥ 2 of 5 operators would pay ≥ $[49]/mo for demand analytics.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Synthetic findings get quoted as real | Tag every finding; badge in UI; generator writes "SYNTHETIC" into quote text prefix |
| Can't recruit operators before Nov 13 | Synthetic operator rows; walk-in script for 3-5 LES venues on an early weeknight |
| Competitor status is stale or wrong | `retrieved_at` per row; `unknown` over guessing; re-check before demo |
| Leading questions bias interviews | Past-behavior questions first; concept test last; peer review of guide |
| PII leaks into repo | IDs only; raw notes stay off-repo; validator scans for emails/phones |
| Synthetic data drives ml-recs weights | Weights from research are labelled placeholders in `docs/synthetic/research.md` |
| Complaint risk mistaken for quality in positioning | Explicit note: complaint risk ≠ nightlife quality; not used as a vibe proxy |
